Semantic search retrieves information based on meaning instead of only matching exact keywords.

Embedding models convert text into numerical vectors. Text with similar meaning tends to have embedding vectors that are closer together.

Cosine similarity can measure similarity between embedding vectors. Search systems can rank stored documents based on their similarity to a query embedding.

Similarity thresholds affect retrieval quality. Lower thresholds can increase recall but may include irrelevant information, while higher thresholds are more selective but may miss useful results.
